"Lost in the middle" — relevance vs position-in-prompt:

  recall
   1.0 │ ●                                            ●
       │  ●                                          ●
   0.8 │   ●                                        ●
       │    ●                                      ●
   0.6 │     ●                                    ●
       │      ●                                  ●
   0.4 │       ● ●  ●  ●  ●  ●  ●  ●  ●  ●  ●  ●
       │
   0.2 │
       │
   0.0 ┼─────────────────────────────────────────────────►
       1     5    10   15   20   25   30   35   40   45  position in prompt

  Beginning and end of the context window:  HIGH attention.
  Middle of the context window:             LOW  attention.

So when you stuff top-10 chunks into the prompt, the LLM is
~25% less likely to use chunk #5 than chunk #1 or chunk #10
— even if #5 is the MOST relevant one your retriever found.

Mitigations: re-rank to put best at start; cap top-k smaller
than instinct says; use re-ranking models that score relevance
not similarity.
