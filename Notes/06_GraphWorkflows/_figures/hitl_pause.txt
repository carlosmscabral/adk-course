──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
key invariants
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- The resume_token uniquely identifies the suspension point. Treat as
  sensitive; bind to user identity before honoring.
- Session state must be in a DURABLE backend if pause can exceed process
  lifetime. InMemorySessionService loses everything on restart — use
  DatabaseSessionService or VertexAiSessionService.
- `rerun_on_resume` is per-node:
    True  for cheap pure functions (router, parser)
    False for expensive LLM calls and side-effecting nodes
- On resume, nodes UPSTREAM of the suspension point are NOT re-paid —
  their outputs replay from the checkpoint.
- Cancellation is handled at the session-service layer: simply do not
  resume, or delete the session entirely. There is no public
  `runner.cancel(invocation_id)` in 2.0 — the App-level lifecycle owns
  abandonment.
