Rule of thumb:
  Dev local + you wrote the prompts:  UnsafeLocal (and only here).
  Math / charts only, no I/O:         BuiltInCodeExecutor.
  General Python in Vertex deploy:    VertexAiCodeExecutor.
  Your infra, your sandbox:           Container or Gke.
  Agent Engine deployment:            AgentEngineSandboxCodeExecutor.
